# Subdivision Boundary Survey

![Project overview image](../assets/images/placeholder-project.png)

## Overview

Designed a complete boundary survey for a subdivision in AutoCAD Civil3D.

**Study Area:** 15-plot suburban subdivision with direct highway access  
**Duration:** Fall 2023  
**Supervisor** Professor Justin Thomas, B.S.

---

## Methods & Tools
**Processing Steps**

1. Drew centerlines and ROW for highways, secondary roads, cul-de-sacs, etc.
2. Outlined subdivision area within square footage constraints
3. Divide subdivision into plots with required acreage, frontage, and setbacks
4. Label plots, roads, monuments, bearings, etc.

**Tools Used**

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| AutoCAD Civil3D (Imperial) | Drafting subdivision in model space |
