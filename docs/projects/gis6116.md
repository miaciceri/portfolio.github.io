# Minimum Critical Area Analysis

![Project overview image](../assets/images/placeholder-project.png)

## Overview

Automated a workflow in ArcGIS Pro ModelBuilder to perform a Minimum Critical Area Analysis from creating species richness maps of different animals in Florida.

**Study Area:** Florida, USA  
**Duration:** Spring 2025  
**Supervisor(s):** Dr. Hartwig Hochmair & Dr. Amr Abd-Elrahman

---

## Methods & Tools

**Data Sources**

- Provided raster datasets of five different animal species' observed locations
- Provided raster datasets of the five species' known preferred habitat locations

**Processing Steps**

1. Create species richness maps by combining each species' observed locations and preferred habitat locations raster datasets
2. Combine all species richness maps together into one joined map for visualization
3. Choose a species to perform a MCA analysis
4. Use a Minimum Area Threshold calculation to determine areas that meet minimum area criteria to support the species

**Tools Used**

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| ModelBuilder | Automate the combining of rasters for every species |
| Raster Calculator | Combining the raster datasets into species richness maps |
| Suitability Modeler | Determine areas suitable to pass the Minimum Area Threshold |
